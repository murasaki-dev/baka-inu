Network and remote access

Neko streams over WebRTC and needs reachable UDP ports or a TURN relay. go2rtc sends the desktop video through an authenticated WebSocket over HTTP, so it can travel through a Cloudflare Tunnel without incoming UDP media ports.

The hostname is a reminder for your Cloudflare dashboard; create the Tunnel and Access policy there. Enable trusted proxy headers only after direct origin access is blocked or restricted to the trusted proxy. Neko also requires the configured UDP range to be forwarded.
